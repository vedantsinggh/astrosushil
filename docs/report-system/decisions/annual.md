# Method Decisions — Annual Reports (Varshphal, Personalised Horoscope)

Report IDs: `varshphal` (VARSHPHAL), `horoscope-annual` (HOROSCOPE). Format as in [`../DECISIONS.md`](../DECISIONS.md). All entries are **provisional until the client signs off**. Each rule is printed under the table that uses it.

| ID | Topic | Decision | Basis | Reference behaviour (not copied) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| VAR-001 | Which year | The Varsha year is the solar-return year **containing the report date** (solar return ≤ report date < next solar return). Its 12 months are Tajik months, not calendar months. | Varshaphala runs birthday to birthday by definition | Calendar Jan–Dec 2026 months, but a Varsha chart for the return of Dec 2026 (inconsistent) |
| VAR-002 | Solar return, Muntha, Panchadhikari, Tajik yogas, Sahams, Harsha Bala | Taken from the engine's `VarshaphalaCalculator` unchanged, cast for the birthplace. Year Lord = office-bearer with a Tajik aspect on the annual Ascendant and the highest strength; the engine measures strength by Harsha Bala (then offices held, then office order) rather than Pancha-vargiya Bala. | Tajika Neelakanthi (B. V. Raman's presentation); engine implementation | Absent (Muntha only, as text) |
| VAR-003 | Months | Masa Pravesha: month *k* begins when the sidereal Sun reaches natal Sun + 30°·(k−1); solved numerically with `TransitIngressSolver`. | Tajika Neelakanthi monthly progression | Calendar months |
| VAR-004 | Mudda dasha | Implemented in-module (`varshphal/calc/mudda.ts`): first lord = remainder of (natal nakshatra no. + completed years − 2) ÷ 9 counted Sun, Moon, Mars, Rahu, Jupiter, Saturn, Mercury, Ketu, Venus; periods = Vimshottari years/120 of the actual year; first period is the natal Moon's remaining share of its nakshatra, its elapsed share closes the year. The engine's `MuddaDashaCalculator` (annual Moon's nakshatra lord, no balance) is **not** used. | Tajika Neelakanthi formula; balance as in standard Varshaphal practice (Raman) | Absent |
| VAR-005 | Favourable / unfavourable transits | Classical gochara from the natal Moon (Phaladeepika ch. 26) without vedha; labelled "Supportive"/"Demanding". Year table also shows the share of days each planet spends in supportive houses (a count). | Classical, deterministic | "Favourable/Unfavourable" flags with no consistent rule |
| VAR-006 | Sahams shown | All engine Sahams except **Mitra Saham**, whose engine formula is identical to Yashas Saham (engine defect, reported). | Avoid printing a known-wrong value | Absent |
| VAR-007 | Monthly emphasis | **No 0–5 ratings.** Each month shows: Mudda lord(s), mid-month positions with houses from Asc and Moon, sign changes, which planets sit in each life area's houses (BPHS bhava significations: work 2/6/10/11, relationships 2/4/5/7, health 1/6/8, inner life 5/9/12), and the count of planets supportive from the Moon. | Transparent facts instead of invented grades | Unexplained 0–5 bar charts; generic month prose |
| VAR-008 | Monthly remedy planet | The Mudda lord running at mid-month; practices are free devotional/ethical acts tied to its weekday. | Uses the year's own timing system | One planet per month, rule undisclosed |
| VAR-009 | Panchang observances | Computed Purnimas of the year (Moon–Sun 180°), Vaishakha Shukla Tritiya span (Akshaya Tritiya), Chaitra Purnima (Hanuman Jayanti, North Indian custom). Amanta month from the Sun's sign at the preceding new moon; Adhika when two consecutive new moons share a sign. Spans are shown as instants; almanacs fix the civil day by sunrise. | Standard Panchanga astronomy | Festival names only, no dates |
| VAR-010 | Avakahada | Engine `AvakhadaChakraCalculator` + birth Panchanga; Varga (name-letter class) omitted because it depends on the name's spelling, not the chart. | Deterministic chart facts only | Included Varga |
| HOR-001 | Horoscope period | The 12 months starting on the 1st of the report month, in three 4-month windows. | Most useful for a report bought at any time of year | Calendar year, Jan–Apr / May–Aug / Sep–Dec |
| HOR-002 | Basis | Transits of Jupiter, Saturn, Rahu, Ketu counted from **both** the natal Moon sign and the natal Ascendant; Venus, Mars, Mercury periods listed. | Classical Rashi horoscope extended with Lagna (product brief) | Moon sign only; house numbers inconsistent with its own sign |
| HOR-003 | Life-area houses | Career 6/10/11, Finance 2/11, Education 4/5/9, Love 5/7, Family 2/4, Health 1/6/8. Slow-planet transits over them are listed per window, not graded. | BPHS bhava significations | Prose only |
| HOR-004 | Lucky associations | From the Moon-sign lord (as D-003 but keyed on the Rashi lord): number, dates reducing to it, weekday, colours, symbolic gem; "use sparingly" colours/dates from its natural enemies (BPHS); Ascendant lord's colours added. | Classical correspondences + naisargika relationships | Sign lookup table with product links |
| HOR-005 | Numerology | Universal year = digit sum of the year; personal year = digit sum of birth day + birth month + year. | Standard numerology | Universal year only |
| HOR-006 | Affirmations | Planet-linked affirmations without neuroscience claims. | Brand: no pseudo-science | "The Science:" neurotransmitter paragraphs |
