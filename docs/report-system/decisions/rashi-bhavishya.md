# Rashi Bhavishya (daily Moon-sign horoscope) — Method Decisions

Feature: free daily horoscope in the app (`GET /api/v1/rashi-bhavishya/today`, `/all`).
Code: `apps/api/src/modules/rashi-bhavishya/` (engine `rashi-gochara.engine.ts`, tables `rashi-gochara.tables.ts`, narrator `rashi-bhavishya.narrator.ts`), scheduler `apps/api/src/services/rashi-bhavishya.scheduler.ts`, mobile `apps/mobile/lib/features/astrology/{models,data,providers,screens}/rashi_bhavishya_*`.
Tests: `apps/api/tests/unit/rashi-gochara-engine.test.ts`, `apps/api/tests/unit/rashi-bhavishya-service.test.ts`, `apps/api/tests/integration/rashi-bhavishya.test.ts`.
The product owner delegated method choices to engineering (2026-11-18). Every choice is **provisional until the client signs off**; `../DECISIONS.md` D-001, D-006 and D-007 apply.

| ID | Topic | Decision | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| RB-001 | What "Rashi Bhavishya" is | A gochara (transit) reading counted from the **Moon sign** (janma rashi). One reading per sign per day, shared by everyone with that Moon sign. It is labelled as not a personal birth-chart reading. | Classical gochara is read from the natal Moon (Phaladeepika ch. 26); this is how Indian daily rashiphal is defined |
| RB-002 | Day and reference instant | The day is the IST calendar date. Positions and panchang are taken at **sunrise at Ujjain** (23.18 N, 75.79 E) on that date; events run to the next sunrise. The sunrise time is shown in the app. If the engine cannot compute sunrise (its 06:00/18:00 fallback), the day fails instead of guessing. | Ujjain is the classical prime meridian of Indian astronomy (Surya Siddhanta); the panchang day starts at sunrise. A shared reading needs one reference place |
| RB-003 | Calculation conventions | Sidereal zodiac, Lahiri ayanamsa, mean nodes, whole signs, via `TransitIngressSolver.getPlanetaryState` and `PanchangCalculator` from `@astro/astrology-engine`. | D-001; existing engine. Note: the solver uses its linear Lahiri approximation, so a planet within a few arcminutes of a sign boundary can differ from other screens |
| RB-004 | House from the Moon sign | Inclusive whole-sign count: the Moon sign itself is the 1st. | Standard gochara counting |
| RB-005 | Favourable houses | Sun 3, 6, 10, 11; Moon 1, 3, 6, 7, 10, 11; Mars 3, 6, 11; Mercury 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 11; Jupiter 2, 5, 7, 9, 11; Venus 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 8, 9, 11, 12; Saturn, Rahu, Ketu 3, 6, 11. Every other house is labelled **"demanding"** (never "bad" or "malefic"). | Phaladeepika ch. 26 (Gochara Phala); same table in Muhurta Chintamani and B. V. Raman, *Hindu Predictive Astrology*. Nodes follow Saturn as in the standard tables |
| RB-006 | Vedha (obstruction) | **Not applied** in this version, and the app says so. | The vedha pairs vary between sources; adding them needs the client's chosen table |
| RB-007 | House and planet meanings | Neutral wording of the bhava significations (BPHS ch. 11–12) and natural karakatva (BPHS ch. 3), e.g. "the 11th house is about gains, friends and fulfilment of wishes". No per-planet-per-house result text is encoded; the LLM may only combine house meaning + favourable/demanding. | Avoids inventing classical results; keeps every statement traceable to a table |
| RB-008 | Events | Sign ingresses and retrograde/direct stations solved by `TransitIngressSolver`: the Moon's change of sign before the next sunrise, and other planets' events in the next 7 days. Ketu's ingress mirrors Rahu's and is not repeated. A planet that changes sign during the day is shown with both houses. | Deterministic, auditable (AGENTS.md §1.3) |
| RB-009 | "Rating" | No score, percentage, lucky number or lucky colour. Only a **plain count** of the nine grahas in favourable vs demanding houses, with the method printed next to it. The old fixtures' lucky number/colour were dropped. | Owner: no fabricated scores. Lucky number/colour have no rule in this method |
| RB-010 | Retrograde | Reported as a fact only (Rahu/Ketu explained as always moving backwards). No retrograde-specific result rules. | Classical opinions on retrograde gochara differ |
| RB-011 | Dates served | Today and tomorrow (IST) are generated; up to 7 past days are served only if already stored. Other dates are a validation error. | Cost control; a horoscope for an arbitrary date is not a product requirement |
| RB-012 | Narrative | Written by the report platform's `LlmNarrator` (Gemini, platform system prompt, claim + editorial validators, retry with feedback) from evidence items built from the facts only, plus a Rashi-specific check that every "planet in sign/house" sentence matches the day and that no ascendant, dasha, lucky value or percentage is mentioned. Three sections: Today's Moon, the wider planetary picture, practical guidance. Failure → standard 503 `RASHI_BHAVISHYA_UNAVAILABLE`, never canned text. | AGENTS.md §1.4; D-006 (no dosha or fear language) |
| RB-013 | Generation and cost | One generation per sign per date (12 LLM calls a day), stored in Postgres (`rashi_daily_horoscopes`, unique per date, sign, language, method and prompt version). The worker prepares today's missing signs every 15 min and tomorrow's from 18:00 IST, with per-date backoff (15 m, 30 m, 1 h, then 2 h). A request generates on demand only if the row is missing; after an on-demand failure that sign/date fails fast for 5 minutes. | Shared content; predictable cost (≈12 × 8k tokens/day) |
| RB-014 | Language | Narrative in English only for now; the screen's labels, facts and method note are bilingual (EN/HI) and the Hindi screen says the detailed text is in English. | A Hindi narrative doubles daily calls; `language` is already part of the storage key, so it can be added later |
| RB-015 | Moon sign of the user | From the active birth profile's backend chart (mobile) or `profileId` / the latest profile (API). No default sign: without a profile the user picks one. The old western sun-sign date ranges ("March 21 – April 19") were removed from the selector because they do not describe a sidereal Moon sign. | No invented defaults |
