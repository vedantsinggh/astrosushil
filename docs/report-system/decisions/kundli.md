# Premium Personalised Kundli — Astrology Method Decisions

Module: `packages/report-engine/src/platform/reports/kundli/`. Applies on top of [`../DECISIONS.md`](../DECISIONS.md) (D-001 to D-008). Every entry is **provisional until the client signs off**. Each table in the report also prints its method under it.

| ID | Topic | Decision | Basis | Reference behaviour (not copied) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| KUN-001 | Information architecture | All 17 reference chapters are covered (see `referencePages` in `kundli.definition.ts`). The 79 per-Antardasha LLM pages are replaced by deterministic Antardasha and Pratyantardasha tables, a per-Mahadasha "at a glance" table, and one narrative per Mahadasha of life (9). There are 45 narrative topics in total, with at most 6 per section. | Cost control. The reference's AD texts are generic per planet pair (analysis §5), so tables plus MD-level narrative carry the same information. | 79 generic AD pages, 9 MD intros |
| KUN-002 | Vara (weekday) | The Vedic weekday runs from sunrise to sunrise. It is the local weekday of the last sunrise at or before birth, using the engine's `calculateSunriseSunset`. The civil weekday is shown separately. When the engine's UTC-based weekday differs, the panchang table says so. | Classical panchang rule (the day begins at sunrise). Engine defect D6 uses `getUTCDay()`. Example: a Toronto birth at 22:40 on a Saturday gets the engine value Sunday and the corrected value Saturday. The engine is not modified (file ownership). | Gave the civil weekday ("Wednesday") for a 04:45 birth, which is before sunrise |
| KUN-003 | Panchang extras | Tithi, nakshatra, yoga and karana are computed at birth and at the day's sunrise, with end times. Bhayat/Bhabhog come from the birth nakshatra's start and end times. Ishta kala = birth − sunrise. 1 ghati = 24 min. LMT = UTC + longitude × 4 min. | Standard panchang arithmetic on engine boundaries | Same items. Its LMT/EoT fields contained garbage values. |
| KUN-004 | Ayana, gola, ritu, samvat | Ayana and gola use the tropical (sayana) Sun: Uttarayana for 270°–90°, Uttara gola for 0°–180°. Ritu comes from the amanta lunar month, two months per season starting Chaitra–Vaisakha = Vasanta. Vikram Samvat is Chaitradi: Gregorian year + 57, or + 56 for Pausha, Magha or Phalguna in January–April. Shaka = Vikram − 135. | Astronomical ayana/gola. Classical lunar-month ritu. Chaitradi Samvat. Uses the engine's amanta-month approximation, which is based on the Sun's sign. | Same fields, plus 8 regional calendars. **Not reproduced:** regional calendars (Punjabi, Bengali, Tamil, Kerala, Nepali, Kartakadi) need a full lunisolar calendar engine. |
| KUN-005 | Avakahada and Ghatak | These come from the engine's `AvakhadaChakraCalculator` and `GhatChakraCalculator`, unchanged. Ghatak is labelled a traditional almanac reference with no warning value (D-006). The Avakahada "Varga" (name-letter class) is omitted because the report has no name-based input. | Engine classical tables | Presented Ghatak as "inauspicious factors" |
| KUN-006 | Sade Sati | Sade Sati is the span when Saturn transits the 12th, 1st and 2nd signs from the natal Moon. Real Saturn ingresses come from `TransitIngressSolver` over the 100 years from birth. Each phase runs from the planet's first entry to its last exit. Stretches split by a retrograde step-back shorter than 3 years are merged into one cycle. A cycle already running at birth is flagged. Phases are not graded. | Classical gochara definition. The engine has only a point-in-time Sade Sati status (README gap). | Contiguous dates for one native. It omitted the cycle running at birth. |
| KUN-007 | Mangal | Uses the engine's `evaluateManglikDosha`: Mars counted from the Ascendant, Moon and Venus, with houses 1/4/7/8/12 (the engine excludes the 2nd house) and the engine's classical exception list. **The engine's severity grade (LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH) is not shown.** Neutral framing per D-006. | Engine (classical rule set) | "Congratulations, you are Manglik", plus an undefined "Anshik" grade. It contradicted its own dosha chapter. |
| KUN-008 | Antardasha facts | For each Antardasha the report shows where its lord sits (house and sign), its sign distance from the Mahadasha lord, and the natural relationship from the Mahadasha lord's side (engine `getNaturalRelationship`, BPHS ch. 15; node relationships are the engine's convention). There is no favourable/unfavourable grade. | BPHS dasha-phala reasoning starts from these relations. Grading formulas are not defined. | Generic text per pair |
| KUN-009 | Divisional charts | 15 vargas plus D1 from the engine: D2, D3, D4, D7, D9, D10, D12, D16, D20, D24, D27, D30, D40, D45, D60. Each caption gives its classical purpose (BPHS ch. 6). **D5, D6, D8 and D11 are not reproduced**: they are not part of the Parashari Shodashavarga and the engine has no formula for them. (The reference's D5 row was a copy of its D30 row.) | Engine (BPHS formulas verified in the audit) | 20 charts, including D5/D6/D8/D11 |
| KUN-010 | Rahu–Ketu ("Kaal Sarp") pattern | Shown only in the neutral "A Note on Doshas" table. "Present" means all seven planets from the Sun to Saturn lie within one 180° arc bounded by the nodes; a planet exactly on a node counts as inside. It is reported as a fact, never as a dosha. | Common modern definition. The engine has no implementation, so it is implemented in `calc/patterns.ts` with tests. | Reference had a generic "no dosh present" page |
| KUN-011 | Ashtakavarga | BAV and SAV come from the engine. Signs with more than 28 SAV bindus (337 / 12 ≈ 28) are highlighted as above average. There is no other scoring. | Classical average benchmark | Table only |
| KUN-012 | Practices (remedies) | The focus planets are the Ascendant lord, the Moon-sign lord and the current Mahadasha lord. For each, the report gives the standard weekday, colour and beej mantra, plus service drawn from the planet's karakatva. **No gemstones, rudraksha, yantra, donations-for-sale or paid rituals** (D-007). | Classical propitiation of the lagna lord and dasha lord; brand philosophy | Trikona-lord gemstones, nakshatra rudraksha, Chandra yantra, and a donation list for one planet with no stated rule |
| KUN-013 | Not reproduced (undefined rules) | Success number, Connection number, Ishtdev deity selection, rudraksha choice and gemstone choice are not reproduced. Instead, the report shows the Karakamsha and the 12th sign from it (Jaimini) as facts for the spiritual chapter, with no deity mapping. | These rules are listed as undefined in README §9, and the deity mappings differ between texts | Printed with no stated formula |
| KUN-014 | Outer planets | Uranus, Neptune and Pluto are not included. | Not part of Parashari analysis. The engine's chart does not compute them for the report. | Placement-only pages for Uranus, Neptune and Pluto |
| KUN-015 | Yogas | Only engine-detected yogas are listed, each with the conditions it verified. The narrative may cite only `yoga.*` evidence. | No hand-added yogas (engine defect D7 avoided) | "7 Raj Yogas" with no rules |
| KUN-016 | KP sub-lord column | Omitted. The planet table shows sign, degree, nakshatra/pada, sign and nakshatra lords, house, dignity, retrograde/combust and speed. | The engine's KP cusps are not Placidus (audit). The reference column was a placeholder. | Sub = "Jup" in every row; Dignity = "--" |
