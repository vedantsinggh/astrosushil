# Method Decisions — Finance Report and Couple Kundli Matching

Same rules as [`../DECISIONS.md`](../DECISIONS.md): engineering chose these methods on 2026-11-18 under the product owner's delegation. All of them are **provisional until the client signs off**. Code: `packages/report-engine/src/platform/reports/{finance,couple-matching,_finance-couple-shared}/`.

| ID | Topic | Decision | Basis | Reference behaviour (not copied) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FIN-001 | Wealth houses | 2nd, 5th, 9th and 11th are the wealth houses (Dhana bhavas). The 1st and 10th are shown with them as their "engines". The 6th, 8th, 12th and 3rd are shown separately as debts, sudden events, outgo and effort. | BPHS (bhava significations); standard Parashari practice | Reference covered only the 2nd house in text |
| FIN-002 | SAV gains vs outgo | Compare Sarvashtakavarga bindus of the 11th and 12th houses. More in the 11th reads as income tending to exceed outgo; otherwise, as a chart that benefits from budgeting. 28 bindus per house is quoted as the average. | Classical Ashtakavarga practice (e.g. BPHS Ashtakavarga chapters; C. S. Patel) | Not in reference |
| FIN-003 | "Gem power %" replaced | Each planet is classified by **functional nature for the Lagna**. Rules apply in this order: (1) the Lagna lord is favourable; (2) a kendra + trikona lord is a Yogakaraka; (3) a trikona lord is favourable, or mixed if it also rules the 3rd, 6th, 8th or 11th; (4) a lord of the 3rd, 6th, 8th or 11th is challenging, except that the Sun and Moon are exempt from 8th-lord blemish; (5) a lord of kendras only, or of 2nd/12th only, is neutral; (6) Rahu and Ketu act through their sign lord. No percentages and no gemstones. The same table replaces the reference's dasha-wise gem table, giving each Mahadasha lord's nature with its wealth (2/5/9/11) and 6/8/12 links. | BPHS ch. 34; Laghu Parashari ch. 2 | Undefined percentage per gem and per dasha, with gem purchase advice |
| FIN-004 | Dhana yogas | Only yogas the engine's `YogaCalculator` detects: category `DHANA` plus `YOGA_LAKSHMI`. Conditions are quoted from the engine evidence. Nothing is added by hand. If none are detected, the report says so calmly. | Brief: engine-detected only | Reference had no yogas |
| FIN-005 | Outlook window | Five calendar years. The window starts in the report year if the report date falls in January–June, otherwise in the next year (2026-11-18 gives 2027–2031). Snapshot transit horizon = 7 years. | Calendar years read best; avoids a near-empty first year | Generation year plus 4 years (2026–2030) |
| FIN-006 | Yearly and monthly finance view | For each year and month: Jupiter and Saturn sign periods (transit ingress solver), house from Lagna and from Moon, classical gochara favourability from the Moon (Jupiter 2/5/7/9/11, Saturn 3/6/11; Phaladeepika ch. 26), "wealth contact" (occupies or aspects the 2nd/5th/9th/11th from Lagna; Jupiter aspects 5/7/9, Saturn 3/7/10), and the running MD–AD. **Vedha is not applied. No scores or grades.** The narrative interprets these facts. | Phaladeepika ch. 26; Parashari drishti | 60 template monthly paragraphs with no stated rule |
| FIN-007 | Hora (D2) | Parashari Hora (Sun's hora = Leo, Moon's hora = Cancer). The planets in each hora and the 2nd lord's hora are reported, with the traditional meaning ("earning through effort" vs "accumulating"). No prediction of amounts. | BPHS ch. 6–7 | Not in reference |
| FIN-008 | Rudraksha / gem remedies | Replaced by ethical, devotional and practical practices tied to the 2nd and 11th lords and the Yogakaraka. Nothing to buy. | D-007 | Rudraksha Kavach, gem weights, wearing rituals |
| FIN-009 | Panchang / Avakahada on the data sheet | Tithi, paksha, yoga and karana from the Sun–Moon elongation at birth. Avakahada from the engine's `AvakhadaChakraCalculator`. **Weekday omitted**, because the engine derives it from the UTC date (known defect, README §5). | Engine modules | Reference used a different engine |
| FIN-010 | Planet table extras | Star lord and KP sub lord are computed on the **Lahiri** longitude (not the KP ayanamsa), and this is stated in the method note. Shadbala ratio and Grah Tara are omitted. Some Tara names (e.g. Naidhana, "death") read as fear. | Engine `KPSubLordCalculator` | KP sub lord, Shadbala ratio and Tara columns |
| FIN-011 | Yogini dasha | Omitted. The engine has no Yogini dasha. | README §5 gaps | Yogini MD list |
| COU-001 | Ashtakoota | The engine's `MatchmakingCalculator` (classical Parashari 36 points) is used unchanged. Bhakoot points are shown raw (0 when the distance is 2/12, 5/9 or 6/8). When an engine exception applies (same lord, mutually friendly lords, same nakshatra), an "effective total" is shown separately. Nadi exceptions follow the engine. | Engine; classical tables | 23.5/36 with a "not severe" note |
| COU-002 | Groom/bride direction | Varna (and the Vashya matrix) are directional. Person A ("groom") is the male partner when the pair is male/female; otherwise it is the person who ordered the report. This is stated under the table. | Classical convention | Male listed first |
| COU-003 | Manglik | The engine's rule is used: Mars in the 1st, 4th, 7th, 8th or 12th from Lagna. Houses from the Moon and from Venus are shown for information. The balancing factors are the engine's. **The engine's severity grade is not displayed.** The wording is neutral ("classical placement", "the classical rule asks for both or neither"), and the brand statement on doshas is printed. | D-006; engine | "Manglik dosh" verdicts |
| COU-004 | Sade Sati / Dhaiya | Saturn sign periods over the 12th, 1st and 2nd (Sade Sati) and the 4th and 8th (Dhaiya) from each Moon, within the 12-year transit horizon. Retrograde re-entries are listed. These are framed as seasons of responsibility. No good/fair/bad result table. | Classical definition; snapshot transits | Three lifetime cycles plus a result table with no rule |
| COU-005 | Kaal Sarp | Presented only as a geometric fact: are all seven planets on one side of the Rahu–Ketu axis? The note explains that the idea is a later tradition, not found in BPHS, and that it is not treated as a dosha. No types, no remedies. | Brand philosophy; no classical source | 12-type essay with remedies |
| COU-006 | 12-year monthly health/finance % graphs | Not reproduced; there is no defined rule. They are replaced by (a) a **shared dasha timeline** (a new row whenever either partner's Antardasha changes, 12 years) and (b) a **joint Jupiter/Saturn transit table** with houses from both Moons and the classical gochara marks (FIN-006 rule). | No classical rule exists for the percentages | Monthly bar charts for 2026–2037 |
| COU-007 | Favourable points and gem selection | Replaced by each person's functional-nature table (FIN-003) and a Mulank/Bhagyank table (D-002). No lucky stones, metals or donation items. | D-003, D-007 | Lookup tables with no stated rule |
| COU-008 | Divisional charts | D2, D3, D4, D7, D9, D10, D12, D16, D30 and D60 for both natives, from the engine's `VargaCalculator`. Bhava Chalit and the KP cusp chart are omitted: the engine's KP cusps are equal houses mislabelled as Placidus (README §5). | Engine | 11 vargas, Chalit, KP cusp chart |
| COU-009 | Friendship table | Naisargika (BPHS ch. 15) and five-fold Panchadha (natural + temporary by sign distance), for the seven classical planets. | Engine `planetary-relationships` | Same |
| COU-010 | Partner evidence and the claim validator (**core change needed**) | The core validator re-checks any "Planet … in Sign" or "Planet … in the Nth house" sentence against the **native's** chart. It also ignores a "Partner:" label prefix, and of the vargas it skips only D9/D10 sentences. Core partner labels ("Partner: Venus is in Leo") and core D2–D60 labels can therefore be rejected falsely. Mitigation: `match.pair.*` summary labels that avoid these forms; native or `match.*` items come first in every topic's evidence list; the writing guidance tells the LLM to describe the partner with "their" and "Venus sign is X". A real-LLM run may still trip the validator until the core is made partner-aware. | Engineering | — |
