# Planet-Transit Family — Method Decisions (Saturn, Jupiter Transit, Rahu–Ketu Transit)

Reports: `saturn` (SATURN), `jupiter-transit` (JUPITER_TRANSIT), `rahu-ketu-transit` (RAHU_KETU_TRANSIT).
Code: `packages/report-engine/src/platform/reports/{saturn,jupiter-transit,rahu-ketu-transit,_planet-transit-shared}/`.
Tests: `packages/report-engine/src/__tests__/platform-planet-transits.test.ts`.
All choices are **provisional until the client signs off**, like `../DECISIONS.md` (D-001 … D-008 apply here too).

| ID | Topic | Decision | Basis | Reference behaviour (not copied) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PT-001 | Source of all transit dates | Every sign period, retrograde re-entry and station is solved with the engine's `TransitIngressSolver` (`findSignIngresses`, `findStationEvents`), same Lahiri sidereal frame as the natal chart. The legacy `saturn-calculator.ts` (hard-coded Sade Sati dates) and static transit tables are **not used**. | Deterministic, auditable, personal (AGENTS.md §1.3) | Fixed date tables; the repo's legacy Saturn module returned the same Virgo-Moon dates for everyone |
| PT-002 | Transit "period" | A period is one pass of the planet through a sign: its main stay plus any retrograde back-and-forth spans of the same sign less than 730 days apart. Each continuous span is printed. The report details the earliest pass not yet finished on the report date ("current") and the pass after it ("next"), plus an overview table (Saturn 30 y, Jupiter 12 y, nodes 19 y). | Classical gochara is read sign by sign; the reference also lists split date ranges | Two periods per report, dates split at retrograde re-entries |
| PT-003 | Sade Sati | Saturn's transit of the 12th (rising), 1st (peak) and 2nd (setting) signs from the **natal Moon sign**. A cycle runs from the first ingress into the 12th to the final egress from the 2nd. All cycles overlapping birth → age 90 are listed; a cycle running at birth is computed back to its real start (window starts 10 years before birth) and flagged. Phases keep their true spans and may overlap by a few months around retrograde re-entries; no rounding to contiguous blocks. Status on the report date is read from the spans. | Standard North Indian definition (Phaladeepika ch. 26 gochara of Saturn from Janma Rashi) | 3 cycles with contiguous phase boundaries; "intensity gauges" with no stated rule (**not reproduced**: no grading) |
| PT-004 | Kantaka Shani and Dhaiya | **Kantaka Shani** = Saturn transiting the 4th, 7th or 10th sign from the Moon ("kantaka" is the classical synonym for kendra; the 1st is already Sade Sati). **Dhaiya** (Small Panoti) = the 4th (Ardhashtama) and 8th (Ashtama) from the Moon. One lifetime table (birth → 90) with each period labelled; the 4th carries both names. | Kantaka = kendra in classical terminology (BPHS, Phaladeepika); Ardhashtama/Ashtama Shani per Phaladeepika ch. 26 | Defined Kantaka as 4/7/10 but tabulated 4/7/8/10 under one label, all three cycles mislabelled "First" |
| PT-005 | Aspects | Whole-sign graha drishti from the engine (`getAspectedSigns`): all 7th; Saturn 3rd/10th; Jupiter 5th/9th; Rahu/Ketu 5th/7th/9th (engine's Phaladeepika convention). Natal and transit aspects list the natal planets in the aspected signs. Method printed under each table. | BPHS ch. 26; engine convention for nodes | Saturn text also claimed an "11th aspect"; Jupiter Cancer section mislabelled aspect houses (**not reproduced**) |
| PT-006 | Ashtakavarga reading of a transit | Planet's own Bhinnashtakavarga bindus in the transit sign: 5–8 supportive, 4 balanced, 0–3 asks for patience. Sarvashtakavarga compared with the average 28 (337/12). Printed as a classical reading, **not a score**. Prastara shows 8 contributors incl. the Ascendant. | BPHS Ashtakavarga chapters; Phaladeepika ch. 23 (four-bindu threshold) | Same 0–8 interpretation idea with unattributed text |
| PT-007 | Functional benefic/malefic | Facts only: houses ruled from the Ascendant with their Parashari categories (kendra, trikona, dusthana, upachaya, dhana) and a yogakaraka check (rules a kendra other than the 1st **and** a trikona other than the 1st). No benefic/malefic verdict computed. | BPHS ch. 34 | Jupiter: "benefic if its signs fall in 1/5/9" (unsourced rule) |
| PT-008 | Nodes | Mean node (D-001). Nodes have no Bhinnashtakavarga (classical scheme uses 7 grahas + Lagna), so node transits show the Sarvashtakavarga of the sign only. A backward node ingress is normal motion, not a "retrograde re-entry". Node aspects per PT-005. Nodes are read through their dispositor (sign lord). | BPHS Ashtakavarga chapters; standard node-dispositor rule | No AV ("fun fact"); true/mean node unspecified |
| PT-009 | Moon-sign "power" ratings | Not reproduced. Instead: the planet's house from the Moon and its natural relationship (BPHS ch. 15) to the Moon-sign lord, as facts. | No classical rule for a "power" rating (README §9) | "Overall Saturn/Jupiter/Rahu Power: Strong/Medium/Weak" |
| PT-010 | Returns | Bonus tables: each re-entry of the planet into its natal sign from birth to age 90 (Saturn ≈ 29.5 y, Jupiter ≈ 12 y, nodes ≈ 18.6 y), from solved ingresses. | Classical gochara over the natal position | — |
| PT-011 | Remedies | Practices only (discipline, service, devotion, mantra from the Navagraha tradition), "nothing to buy". Gemstone, rudraksha, crystal pages and Rahu gem-eligibility rule dropped (D-007). Astro-Vastu direction mapping is undefined (README §9), replaced by a living-space practice topic. | D-006, D-007 | Gem/rudraksha/crystal recommendations, coupons, vastu directions |
| PT-012 | Mundane ("global impact") pages | Narrative topic restricted to symbolic collective themes of the transit sign; explicitly no forecasts of world events, markets or people. | Avoids LLM-inferred predictions (AGENTS.md §1.4) | Specific world predictions (crypto, migration, AI…) |
| PT-013 | Celebrity case studies (Saturn ref 76–82) | Not reproduced. Replaced by "How Saturn Builds Your Strength" from the reader's own chart. | Real people's charts cannot be verified; synthetic/own data only | Four celebrity portraits with unverified Sade Sati claims |
| PT-014 | Mythology and verses | Static educational blocks in our own words; verses are the public-domain Navagraha Stotra (attributed to Vyasa) and standard Navagraha name mantras, with our own translations. | Public tradition | Different shlokas; Durga/Bhairav mantras for nodes |
