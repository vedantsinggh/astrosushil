## 4. Required astrological data

- Natal Rahu and Ketu: house, sign, nakshatra. These drive 27-way nakshatra, 12-way house and 12-way sign text for each node.
- Houses at 5th/7th/9th from each node (nodes are given Jupiter-like 5/7/9 aspects), with house-themed text.
- **Conjunctions:** planets sharing the node's house, with a "stands alone" fallback. The sample shows Ketu + Venus in 1st.
- **Moon-sign rating:** natal Moon sign → "Overall Rahu/Ketu Power" (Strong/Medium/Weak with coloured emoji) + text.
- **Karmic axis:** past life / challenges / solutions keyed to node house/sign.
- **Transit:** node sign-ingress dates (true/mean node unspecified): 18 May 2025 → Aquarius/Leo; 5 Dec 2026 → Capricorn/Cancer; to 23 Jun 2028. These are single continuous ranges, since nodes do not re-enter.
  - Transit house from lagna (Rahu 4th / Ketu 10th, then 3rd / 9th) + 5/7/9 aspects from the transit house.
  - Transit house from Moon (Rahu in Aquarius = 6th from Virgo; Ketu in Leo = 12th).
  - **Transit-over-natal-planets check:** whether any natal planet sits in the transit sign (conjunction by sign), with a fallback text.
  - "Spheres of life": text keyed to ascendant sign × transit period, and Moon sign × period.
- **Gemstone eligibility rule:** Rahu house ∈ {1, 3, 6, 11} → suitable. Otherwise not suitable. The Ketu stone is described regardless.
- **Astro-Vastu:** node house → problem directions.
- No dashas, AV, yogas, sade sati or divisional charts.
