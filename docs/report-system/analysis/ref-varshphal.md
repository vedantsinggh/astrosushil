# Reference Forensics: Varshphal Report (2026)

Source: `docs/referencerepots/Varshphal Report.pdf`. Chromium/Skia, A4, **97 pages**. Text layer on all pages except the cover (p1), the 12 month-divider pages (p20, 26, 32, 38, 44, 50, 56, 62, 68, 74, 80, 86) and the back cover (p97). Those carry only title text in images, which was OCR'd. No scanned pages.

- **Year:** 2026. Monthly sections cover **January–December 2026** (calendar months, not birthday-to-birthday).

  - Natal Lagna (p8).

These positions correspond to **about late December 2026**, i.e. the solar return of the year starting Dec 2026, while the monthly content covers Jan–Dec 2026. **Mismatch/ambiguity.**

- **Monthly bar chart (×12):** 6 horizontal bars, salmon → rust gradient on a pale track, 0–5 axis, dashed gridlines. Integer ratings (e.g. Jan: Career 4, Love 4, Health 4, Family 3, Education 5, Spiritual 3).

## 4. Required astrological data

- **Birth synopsis / Avakhada Chakra:** nakshatra + pada, nakshatra lord, Panchang at birth (tithi not shown; yoga, karana), Gana, Yoni, Nadi, Varna, Vashya, Varga, Yunja, Hansak (tatva), Sun sign tropical, lagna lord, rashi lord.
- **Natal D1** with degrees and retrograde flags. Outer planets are included.
- **Transit chart** at the generation date. The **transit house of each of 9 planets from natal lagna** drives the p10–18 text (Sun 6th, Moon 11th, Mercury 5th, Mars 5th, Venus 7th, Jupiter 8th, Saturn 5th, Rahu 4th, Ketu 10th).
- **Favourable/Unfavourable flag per planet.** The rule is **not stated** and does not match classical Gochara from the Moon consistently: Rahu in 6th from Moon is marked Unfavourable, Saturn in 7th from Moon is marked Favourable. **Ambiguous: needs a spec.**
- **Varshphal (Tajik) chart:** solar-return moment (Sun returns to natal sidereal longitude) → annual lagna + planets. **Muntha house** (natal lagna + completed years; here 8th from the varsha lagna, consistent with 43 completed years: Scorpio + 43 signs = Gemini = 8th from Scorpio). Muntha-house text (12-way).
- **Absent despite the "Varshphal" label:** Varsheshwar/Year Lord, Panchadhikari, Tajik yogas (Ithasala, Ishrafa, etc.), Sahams (Punya etc.), Mudda/Patyayini dasha, Harsha bala, Tri-pataki. The report is essentially monthly horoscope content, not a classical Tajik report.
- **Monthly ratings 0–5 for 6 areas:** basis undisclosed (**ambiguous**). The monthly prose contains **no planetary references at all** (no planets, houses or transits), so it is generic text likely selected by rating/area.
- **Monthly remedies:** each month's remedies key on a single planet via weekday/colour:
  - Jan Mercury (Wednesday/green), Feb Moon (Monday/white), Mar Sun (Sunday/red), Apr Mercury, May Venus (Friday/white), Jun Mars (Tuesday), Jul Jupiter (Thursday/yellow), Aug Saturn, Sep Saturn, Oct Jupiter, Nov Mars, Dec Venus.
  - The planet-selection rule is undisclosed. It could be the month's afflicting transit or a Mudda dasha lord. **Ambiguous.**
- **Panchang remedies:** festival names only. No computed dates.

## 5. Content depth

- About **14,300 words** total. Monthly blocks average about 750–850 words each (×12 ≈ 9,500 words, 66% of the report).
- Each life area gets 2 paragraphs (about 100–150 words).
- Planet pages: about 100–135 words.
- **Personalised vs generic:**
  - Template-personalised: birth synopsis, charts, the 9 transit-house planet pages, Muntha text, ratings, remedy planet choice (about 25%).
  - Monthly prose is generic, everyday-life filler (about 65%).
  - Front/back matter about 10%.
  - Prose is oddly mundane and sometimes repetitive ("Laundry duties this month get shared fairly…", hand-grip exercises).
- Excerpts:
  - p19: "With Muntha in the Eighth House, emotions may feel fragile this year. Even small setbacks could hit harder than expected, leaving you feeling drained or overwhelmed."
  - p22: "Laundry duties this month get shared fairly as your partner helps wash, fold, and put away clothes without being reminded constantly about household responsibilities."
  - p25: "❖ Keep a decision journal: list facts, options, final choice, and what you will measure one week later."

## 6. Boilerplate vs unique

- **Vendor boilerplate:** disclaimer, author note, best-way-to-study, index, closing, caution-on-remedies, promo banners.
- **Unique:**
  - Avakhada synopsis (shared with the Personalised Horoscope family).
  - Planet transit-house pages with favourable flags.
  - Varshphal chart + Muntha.
  - 12-month rating/prose/remedy blocks.
  - Panchang festival remedies.
- **No remedy suite** (gem/rudraksh/vastu) like the transit reports.
