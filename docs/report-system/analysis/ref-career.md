- **Aspects onto houses 1, 2, 6, 7, 10 and 11:** full 7th aspect plus special aspects (Mars 4/8, Jupiter 5/9, Saturn 3/10, **Rahu/Ketu 5/9**). The report also uses a "Sun 10th special aspect" (non-standard ⚠).
- **D10:** lagna sign; lords of the 1st, 3rd, 5th, 6th, 7th, 8th, 9th, 10th, 11th and 12th with placements; occupants; Sun and Saturn D10 house; Rahu D10 house; "Saturn afflicted" test.
- **Jaimini Chara Karakas:** Atmakaraka (highest degree: Mars 28.86°) and Amatyakaraka (second highest: Moon 27.18°). Uses the 7-karaka scheme (Rahu excluded), consistent with the Kundli report.
- **Vimshottari:** birth Mahadasha (Mars); current MD (Saturn) with houses ruled; MD and AD date ranges for specific planets (Venus, Mercury). Depth used is **MD plus AD**.
- **Yogas:** Budha-Aditya, Kendra-Trikona, Dhan, Voshi (Vesi/Vasi), Chandra-Mangal, Gajakesari (S3, S11).
- **BNN (Bhrigu Nandi Nadi):** Saturn as karma karaka; Saturn's house, sign and aspects; Saturn's 10th-aspect target planets; **the "Saturn chain"** (the next planet Saturn "moves toward").
- **Ashtakavarga:** BAV for 7 planets and SAV per sign; comparisons of SAV 6th vs 7th and 11th vs 10th.
- **Transits:** current and next sign of Saturn, Jupiter, Rahu and Ketu, with ingress/egress dates and durations (needs an ephemeris sign-ingress search).
- **Remedies:** gemstone by 11th lord; rudraksha (generic list); Lakshmi mantra; astro-vastu directions per house.
- **Not used:** shadbala, nakshatra (only in the header), D9, numerology, sade sati, doshas.
