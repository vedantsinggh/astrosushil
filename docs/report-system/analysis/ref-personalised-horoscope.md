- **Brand:** third-party "Astro Arun Pandit Private Limited". Its footer has a WhatsApp/support line with hours. **Structural reference only.**
- **Language:** English only, no Devanagari.
- **Sample personal data:** only **name + Moon sign (Virgo)**.
  - No birth date/time/place, no chart, no nakshatra.

## 3. Tables, charts, visuals

- **No astrological charts, no tables** (no birth details table, no kundali, no transit chart, no ashtakavarga).
- **Visuals:**
  - Every page has a tall **arched (cathedral-window) frame** in thin brown line, topped by a circular **Virgo-maiden line-art medallion** with sparkles.
  - Section dividers use single-line brown illustrations: businessman with briefcase (Career), money in a pocket (Finance), and similar ones for each area; a colour-swatch mosaic (Colours); a calendar "2026 XX" (Dates); a crystal in an arch (Gemstones); "Celestials" crystal-in-hands (Remedies); a numerology wheel with digits and stars (Numerology).
  - Caps titles in brown with a rule line.
  - Inline blue hyperlinks (Moon Sign Calculator, Emerald ring, Peridot pendant, Green Tourmaline jewelry, Virgo Lagna Puja).

## 4. Required astrological data

- **Moon sign only** (or a name-syllable fallback). All text is per-sign template content (12 variants × year).
- **Annual sign-level transits referenced in prose:**
  - Jupiter's 2026 sign changes (11th → 12th → 1st with "November 1st" as a date). This matches Jupiter entering Leo ~31 Oct 2026, which is the 12th from Virgo, not the 1st. **The text is internally inconsistent for Virgo.**
  - Saturn "10th house throughout 2026" (Saturn in Pisces is 7th from Virgo).
  - Rahu 10th / Ketu 4th (Rahu in Aquarius is 6th from Virgo).
  - The house numbers appear copied from another sign's template (the Jupiter numbers fit Leo). **The reference has errors; do not replicate.**
- **Numerology:** universal year number (digit sum of the year), sign's lucky number (Virgo → 5) → lucky dates (5/14/23) and unlucky dates (8/17/26 → number 8).
- **Sign attributes:** ruling planet (Mercury), colours, gemstones (Emerald + substitutes), weekday remedies.
- No dashas, nakshatra, ashtakavarga, yogas, sade sati status or divisional charts.

## 5. Content depth

- About **10,250 words**. Life-area forecast pages: about 250–290 words in 4–5 paragraphs, plus "In Short" summaries (3 bullets).
- Planet pages: about 200–235 words.
- **Personalisation:** about 0% chart-specific. 100% Moon-sign template + name. Generic/educational vs sign-specific is roughly 30/70.
- Tone: friendly, motivational, reassuring ("Ready for a year of positive changes?"), long sentences, some pseudo-science.
- Excerpts:
  - p7: "Ready for a year of positive changes? 2026 is bringing you a nice mix of opportunities and growth, with Jupiter working in your favor throughout most of the year."
  - p18: "Jan-April: Struggles early on, but focus and patience may help you overcome challenges. May- August: New career opportunities… Sept- Dec: Boost in confidence and success…"
  - p59: "The Science: Mercury governs your core communication and analytical abilities, triggering the release of acetylcholine and dopamine when you affirm your inner strengths."

## 6. Boilerplate vs unique

- **Boilerplate:**
  - About page, Disclaimer (legal, different from the transit reports' "cosmic" disclaimer).
  - Moon-sign explanation.
  - Remedies/do's & don'ts structure.
  - Affirmations "How to use".
  - Conclusion/Promise letter, IP back cover.
  - Footer contact strip.
- **Unique to this product:**
  - Life-area forecasts with 4-month "In Short" buckets.
  - Colours, lucky/unlucky dates, numerology, affirmations with "The Science".
  - Personality trait list.
- **No** gem/rudraksh/vastu/mantra suite like the transit reports.

## 7. Visual identity

- **Colours:** pure white page; **warm brown/bronze (#8B5A2B-ish)** line art, frame, titles and footer text; black body text. Cover: muted teal/grey painterly illustration with white serif title.
- **Fonts:** Playfair Display (titles), Yeseva One (accents), Be Vietnam Pro Light/Regular/Medium/SemiBold (body).
- **Header:** none other than the arched frame + medallion.
