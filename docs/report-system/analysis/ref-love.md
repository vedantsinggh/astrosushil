## 5. Content depth

- Total is about 4,070 words.
- Interpretive sections run 1–3 paragraphs of 60–110 words each, about 150–250 words per topic. Bullet-based topics (Darakaraka, Mulank, care tips, remedies) have 3–6 one-line bullets.
- Proportions:
  - Generic or front/back matter (disclaimer, author, study guide, section intros, epilogue, gifts, closing, tithi table): about **45%**.
  - Placement-selected lookup paragraphs (lagna, Moon, Venus house, Venus conjunction, 5th sign, 7th sign, DK planet and house, Mulank): about **45%**.
  - Computed data (tables and timing): about **10%**.
- The tone is a light, playful lifestyle voice with everyday imagery ("perfect selfie", "morning chai ritual", "favourite playlist").
- Excerpts:
  - p8: "Since Scorpio is in your first house, your love life is intense, passionate, and full of mystery. You love deeply, and when you're in a relationship, it's all or nothing, you don't do anything halfway."
  - p13: "Your marriage feels like a cozy home-cooked meal after a long day, secure, nourishing, and satisfying."
  - p15: "Your spouse will be the perfect blend of wisdom and humor—offering life advice one moment and cracking a joke about how they can't fold laundry properly the next!"
- **Defects seen:**
  - "Darkarak/darkaraka" misspelled.
  - The p15 bullets are out of order: the text interleaves, which suggests a layout bug.
  - p18 tithi lists repeat "Navami".
  - p17 lucky dates (10, 15, 19, 24) do not match Mulank 1 rules (1, 10, 19, 28) in an obvious way.
