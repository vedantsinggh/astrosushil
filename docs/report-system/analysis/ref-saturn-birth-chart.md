## 4. Required astrological data

- Natal Saturn: house, sign, nakshatra, dignity (exalted in Libra mentioned). Text lookups by house (12), sign × house (Saturn Impact / Karmic Lesson), nakshatra (27), house archetype (benefic/malefic by house).
- **Natal aspects:** 3rd/7th/10th from Saturn with sign names. The generic text also claims an 11th aspect, which is used in the transit pages.
- **Conjunctions:** the sample says "Saturn and Ketu" although they are in different houses (Libra 12th vs Scorpio 1st). **This is a reference inconsistency.**
- Moon sign: Saturn-Moon relationship text (12-way), Moon-sign "Overall Saturn Influence" rating.
- **Ashtakavarga:** Saturn BAV with the 8 contributors (including Lagna), SAV per-planet matrix, Saturn bindus in a given sign (here the lagna sign).
- **Sade Sati:**
  - Compute all Saturn transits through 12th/1st/2nd from the natal Moon across the lifetime (3 cycles shown, birth 1983 → 2073). Dates use the first ingress of the 12th and the final egress of the 2nd. The sample uses contiguous phase boundaries (end of one = day before next).
  - Current Sade Sati status.
  - Phase intensity meter (gauge). The rule is undefined in the reference: **ambiguous, needs a spec**.
  - Year-by-year text (Year 1, 2, 2.5) per phase keyed to Moon sign.
- **Life Overview:** text keyed to Moon sign (Virgo) × 8 life areas.
- **Transit:**
  - Saturn sign periods including retrograde back-and-forth: Pisces 29 Mar 2025 – 3 Jun 2027; Aries 3 Jun 2027 – 20 Oct 2027 + 23 Feb 2028 – 8 Aug 2029.
  - **Retrograde station dates:** 27 Jul 2026 – 11 Dec 2026; 20 Oct 2027 – 23 Feb 2028. The retrograde text is keyed separately.
  - Transit house **from Moon** (Pisces = 7th from Virgo) + aspects 3/7/10/11.
  - Transit-over-natal-planets check with a fallback.
- **Kantak Shani:** Saturn transits in the 4th/7th/8th/10th from the Moon for 3 cycles (the 8th is Ashtama Shani, included under Kantak). Effects text from the Moon and from the Ascendant; remedies per house.
- **Dhaiya (Small Panoti):** not named separately. The 4th/8th from Moon appears under Kantak Shani.
- No Vimshottari/Saturn dasha (it is mentioned only in remedy text, "Saturn's Transit, Phases or Dashas"). No yogas.
- **Remedies:** gem list (Blue Sapphire + substitute + Diamond/Venus, probably a yogakaraka/friend logic); rudraksha 7 and 14 mukhi; vastu direction keyed to Saturn house.
