## 1. Identity, language, sample data

- **Title (cover p1):** "JUPITER TRANSIT REPORT", with the client name in a pill box. The cover footer reads "G O A T: Gemsmantra | Occult Gurukul | Astro Arun Pandit | The Pooja House".
- **Brand:** a third-party competitor, "Astro Arun Pandit Private Limited". The footer on every content page gives its services, phone numbers, website and email. **Do not copy the brand. Treat it as structural reference only.**
- **Language:** English throughout. The only Devanagari is a Sanskrit shloka on p8 (बृहस्पतिः सुरगुरुः …, followed by an English translation) and the mantra on p67 (ॐ बृहस्पतये नमः, with transliteration, pronunciation and meaning). Hinglish terms appear without translation: kundali, bhav, rashi, Moonga, Pukhraj, Moti, desi ghee diya.
- **Text extraction:** about 39 of 79 pages return no text. 13 of these are full-bleed decorative Jupiter photos (p5, 11, 17, 23, 30, 34, 39, 42, 55, 60, 64, 70, 75). The rest are text rasterised as images (p3, 4, 6–10, 15, 16, 35, 47, 48, 51, 56, 58, 59, 69, 71–74, 76, 78, 79), which were OCR'd. Nothing is scanned: all pages are generated designs, but some are flattened to images.
- **Sample client (reference data only):** Puneet Kumar, DOB 28/12/1983, 04:45, Bulandshahr, Uttar Pradesh. Moon sign Virgo, nakshatra Chitra. Jupiter in house 2, Sagittarius, Mula nakshatra. The Lagna chart implies a Scorpio ascendant (As 9°).
- **Report "as-of" framing:** a 2026 report. The transit chart is dated April 30, 2026. It covers Jupiter in Gemini (Jan 1 – Jun 2, 2026) and in Cancer (Jun 2, 2026 – Jun 26, 2027).

## 4. Required astrological data per section

- **Birth details:** Moon sign, Moon nakshatra, Jupiter house (from lagna), Jupiter rashi, Jupiter nakshatra.
- **Charts:** sidereal positions of all 9 grahas + Ascendant with degrees in sign. Outer planets Uranus/Neptune/Pluto also appear. House-sign layout for D1 and Chandra lagna. Transit positions on a report reference date.
- **Nakshatra section:** natal Jupiter nakshatra (27-way text lookup).
- **House section:** natal Jupiter house (12-way lookup).
- **Natal aspects:** houses at 5th/7th/9th from natal Jupiter (here 6, 8, 10). There is per-aspected-house text (12-way lookup).
- **Benefic/Malefic:** which houses Sagittarius and Pisces occupy from lagna. Rule: Jupiter-owned signs in houses 1/5/9 make it a functional benefic, with "gifts" text per owned-sign-in-house.
- **Rashi:** natal Jupiter sign (12-way lookup).
- **Conjunctions:** planets in the same house as Jupiter. The sample text says "Jupiter, Sun, and Moon together", but the chart shows Jupiter with Sun + Mercury, and the Moon is in Virgo. **This is an inconsistency in the reference and should not be replicated.**
- **Moon sign psyche:** natal Moon sign gives an "Overall Jupiter Power" rating (Weak/Medium/Strong label) plus text.
- **Transit windows:** Jupiter sign-ingress dates including retrograde re-entries, shown as multiple date ranges per sign:
  - Gemini: 14 May 2025 – 18 Oct 2025, then 5 Dec 2025 – 2 Jun 2026.
  - Cancer: 2 Jun 2026 – 31 Oct 2026, then 25 Jan 2027 – 26 Jun 2027.
  - The gap periods are the Cancer and Gemini retrograde back-and-forth.
- **Transit house from lagna** (Gemini = 8th from Scorpio): Physical/Mental/Spiritual text per house.
- **Transit aspects** (houses 5/7/9 from transit house). The sample's Cancer section mislabels them as 1st/5th/7th; the correct houses are 1st/3rd/5th from the 9th. **This is a reference inconsistency.**
- **Transit house from Moon** (Gemini = 10th from Virgo): text, plus the transit's 5th/7th/9th aspects from the Moon.
- **Ashtakavarga:**
  - Jupiter BAV contributions per planet.
  - Full 7×12 BAV matrix with SAV totals.
  - Bindu-score interpretation (0–8) for the transit sign.
  - Separate text "As per Ascendant" and "As per Moon Sign" for the transit year.
- **No** dashas, sade sati, yogas or divisional charts.
- **Remedies:**
  - Gemstone choice uses a rule-like "suggested gemstones" list (Red Coral, Yellow Sapphire, Pearl, probably lords of trines from Scorpio lagna: Mars (1), Jupiter (5), Moon (9)), with a 6/8/12 placement avoidance rule.
  - Astro-Vastu directions keyed to the Jupiter house.
  - Everything else is generic.

## 5. Content depth

- Total ≈ 6,400 words of extractable text + ≈ 4,700 OCR words ≈ **11,000 words**. Image pages make up about 16% of the page count.
- Typical content page: 150–350 words in bullet lists of 4–13 bullets, with each bullet 1–2 sentences (15–35 words). Paragraph blocks are rare (intros of 2–4 paragraphs).
- **Personalised vs generic:** about 40% personalised (text selected by placement: p12–14, 18–33, 35–54) and about 60% generic (front matter, mythology, mundane, ashtakavarga explainers, all remedies except the gem list and vastu directions). The "personalised" text is template lookup text with the name prefixed ("Puneet Kumar Your Jupiter in Mula…"). There is no space after the name, which is a template seam.
- Excerpts:
  - p20: "❍ You've got financial fairy dust sprinkled all over you! Jupiter's blessing makes your wallet happier than most, this placement is especially auspicious for wealth matters."

- Tone: chatty, second-person, Gen-Z/pop ("hello, nepotism!", "Guest Central"), many exclamation marks.
