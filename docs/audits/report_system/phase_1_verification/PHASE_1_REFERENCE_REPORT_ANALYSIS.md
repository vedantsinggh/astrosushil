2. **Legal & Ethical Framing:** Prominent, multi-paragraph **Disclaimer** on page 2 or 3 explicitly framing astrology as an interpretive navigation tool rather than fatalistic prediction.
3. **Personal Greeting:** **Message from the Author** explaining the personal philosophy of astrology (choice, karma, action) and guidance on "How to Study This Report".
4. **Navigation Index:** Table of Contents / Journey Map with chapter numbering and conceptual descriptions.
5. **Technical Blueprint:** Comprehensive Panchang, Planetary Longitude tables, Lagna/Chalit charts, and Navamsha (D9) or relevant divisional charts.
6. **Detailed Synthesis Chapters:** House-by-house, planet-by-planet, or transit-by-transit deep-dives combining categorical traits, constructive expressions, and risk factors.
7. **Temporal Roadmaps:** Multi-year or monthly predictive timelines.
8. **Actionable Remedies & Closing:** Ethical, non-fear-based remedies (gemstones, mantras, charity, psychological lifestyle changes) concluding with author sign-off.
