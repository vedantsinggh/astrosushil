  \CardTitle{1. Our Approach to Jyotish: Cosmic Weather, Not Fatalism}
  {\fontsize{8.5}{12}\selectfont
  Vedic astrology (\textit{Jyotish}, literally ``the Science of Light'') is an ancient symbolic, astronomical, and probabilistic navigational tradition. In the classical tradition of Maharishi Parashara, a natal horoscope maps the cosmic conditions and psychological archetypes active at the precise moment of birth. Classical philosophy draws an essential distinction between \textbf{Prarabdha Karma} (the initial conditions, environmental baseline, and accumulated momentum of past actions) and \textbf{Purushartha} (conscious human effort, moral discernment, and sovereign free will). Jyotish does not pronounce an unalterable fate; it illuminates the prevailing energetic weather so that you may exercise your Purushartha with heightened self-knowledge, dignity, and strategic foresight.\par}
